Academic Project Initiative

Empowering Next-Generation Engineers

StudentHub is the official semester-long full-stack web project developed for the subject Web Development Frameworks (ITUE203) in the Faculty of Technology & Engineering (FTE) at Charotar University of Science and Technology (CHARUSAT).

🎯

Our Vision

To provide undergraduate engineering students with an intuitive, unified, and real-time digital gateway for academic monitoring, event registration, assignment management, and secure communication.

⚡

Core Objectives

Demonstrate mastery across modern frontend engineering (Semantic HTML5, CSS Grid/Flexbox, DOM Interactivity, Canvas CAPTCHA) and secure backend architecture (PHP POST processing, sanitization, CSV/JSON file locking).
